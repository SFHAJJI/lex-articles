### Document

7.6.2016 EN Official Journal of the European Union C 202/95 Article 115 (ex Article 94 TEC) Without prejudice to Article 114, the Council shall, acting unanimously in accordance with a special legislative procedure and after consulting the European Parliament and the Economic and Social Committee, issue directives for the approximation of such laws, regulations or administrative provisions of the Member States as directly affect the establishment or functioning of the internal market.
