<a id="art_1"></a>

### Article 1

By way of derogation from Article 193 of Directive 2006/112/EC, Latvia is authorised to continue to designate the recipient as the person liable to pay VAT in the case of timber transactions.

<a id="art_2"></a>

### Article 2

This Decision shall apply from 1 January 2010 until ►M1 31 December 2015** ◄ **.

<a id="art_2a"></a>

### Article 2a

Any request for the extension of the measure provided for in this Decision shall be submitted to the Commission by 31 March 2015 and shall be accompanied by a report on the application of that measure.

<a id="art_3"></a>

### Article 3

This Decision is addressed to the Republic of Latvia.
