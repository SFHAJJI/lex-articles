### art_1

Article 1

Article 30(1) of Directive 2004/17/EC is not applicable to bituminous coal mining in the Czech Republic. Consequently, Directive 2004/17/EC shall continue to apply to contracts awarded by contracting entities and intended to enable them to carry out such activities in the Czech Republic.

<a id="art_2"></a>

### art_2

Article 2

This Decision is addressed to the Czech Republic.
