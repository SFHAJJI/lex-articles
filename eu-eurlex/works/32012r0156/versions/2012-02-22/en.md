### art_1

Article 1

Annexes I to IV to Regulation (EC) No 44/2001 are replaced by the text in the Annex to this Regulation.

<a id="art_2"></a>

### art_2

Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the 20th day following its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.
