### Document

Official Journal of the European Union EN Series L 2014/25 3.11.2023 Corrigendum to Directive 2014/25/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 26 February 2014 on procurement by entities operating in the water, energy, transport and postal services sectors and repealing Directive 2004/17/EC ( Official Journal of the European Union L 94 of 28 March 2014 ) 1. On page 306, Article 55(1), first subparagraph: for: ‘1. Member States may provide that contracting entities may acquire works, supplies and/or services from a central purchasing body offering the centralised purchasing activity referred to in point (a) of point (10) of Article 2.’ ,read: ‘1. Member States may provide that contracting entities may acquire supplies and/or services from a central purchasing body offering the centralised purchasing activity referred to in point (a) of point (10) of Article 2.’ . 2. On page 348, Annex XI, Part A, point 11(a): for: ‘(a) Final date for receipt of tenders or indicative tenders where a dynamic purchasing system is introduced;’, read: ‘(a) Final date for receipt of tenders where a dynamic purchasing system is introduced;’.
