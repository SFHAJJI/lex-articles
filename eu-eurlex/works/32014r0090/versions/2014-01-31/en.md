<a id="art_1"></a>

### art_1

Article 1

Decanoic acid shall be approved as an active substance for use in biocidal products for product-types 4, 18 and 19, subject to the specifications and conditions set out in the Annex.

<a id="art_2"></a>

### art_2

Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.
