### art_1

Article 1

The pairs of currencies that meet the requirements of Article 354(1) of Regulation (EU) No 575/2013 are set out in the Annex to this Regulation.

<a id="art_2"></a>

### art_2

Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.
