<a id="art_1"></a>

### art_1

Article 1

In Article 8(1) of Directive 2014/23/EU, the amount ‘EUR 5 225 000’ is replaced by ‘EUR 5 548 000’.

<a id="art_2"></a>

### art_2

Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on 1 January 2018.
