### Document

29.3.2019 EN Official Journal of the European Union L 91/77 Corrigendum to Directive (EU) 2018/957 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 28 June 2018 amending Directive 96/71/EC concerning the posting of workers in the framework of the provision of services ( Official Journal of the European Union L 173 of 9 July 2018 ) On page 22, Article 1, point (2)(c), second subparagraph, second sentence: for: ‘The employer shall, without prejudice to point (h) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 1, reimburse the posted worker for such expenditure in accordance with the national law and/or practice applicable to the employment relationship.’, read: ‘The employer shall, without prejudice to point (i) of the first subparagraph of paragraph 1, reimburse the posted worker for such expenditure in accordance with the national law and/or practice applicable to the employment relationship.’.
