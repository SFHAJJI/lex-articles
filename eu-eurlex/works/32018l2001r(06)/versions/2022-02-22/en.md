### Document

22.2.2022 EN Official Journal of the European Union L 41/37 Corrigendum to Directive (EU) 2018/2001 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 December 2018 on the promotion of the use of energy from renewable sources ( Official Journal of the European Union L 328 of 21 December 2018 ) On page 139, Article 35(3): for: ‘3. The power to adopt delegated acts referred to in the fifth subparagraph of Article 7(3) shall be conferred on the Commission for a period of two years from 24 December 2018.’, read: ‘3. The power to adopt delegated acts referred to in the fifth subparagraph of Article 7(3) shall be conferred on the Commission until 31 December 2021.’.
