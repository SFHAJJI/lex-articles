### Document

Official Journal of the European Union EN Series L 2020/1784 2.2.2024 Corrigendum to Regulation (EU) 2020/1784 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 25 November 2020 on the service in the Member States of judicial and extrajudicial documents in civil or commercial matters (service of documents) ( Official Journal of the European Union L 405 of 2 December 2020 ) On page 56, Article 33(3): for: ‘3. The Commission shall publish the information communicated in accordance with paragraph 1 in the Official Journal of the European Union, with the exception of the addresses and other contact details of the agencies and of the central bodies and the geographical areas in which they have jurisdiction.’, read: ‘3. The Commission shall publish the information communicated in accordance with paragraph 1 through appropriate means, including through the European e-Justice Portal.’.
