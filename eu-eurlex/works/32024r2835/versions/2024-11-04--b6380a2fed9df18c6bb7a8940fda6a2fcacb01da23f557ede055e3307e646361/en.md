<a id="art_1"></a>

### art_1

Article 1

1. For the purpose of complying with their transparency reporting obligations pursuant to Articles 15, 24 and 42 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, providers of intermediary services, providers of hosting services, providers of online platforms, providers of very large online platforms and providers of very large online search engines shall use the CSV- or XLSX-version of the templates set out under Annex I to this Regulation.

2. The templates referred to in paragraph 1 of this Article shall be filled in and made publicly available in accordance with Annex II of this Regulation.

<a id="art_2"></a>

### art_2

Article 2

1. The yearly reporting period for providers of intermediary services, of hosting services, and of online platforms pursuant to Article 15(1) of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 shall be from 1 January until 31 December.

2. Providers of very large online platforms and of very large online search engines shall publish their transparency reports at least every six months pursuant to Article 42(1) of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, covering respectively periods from 1 January until 30 June and 1 July until 31 December.

3. Providers of intermediary services, providers of hosting services, providers of online platforms, providers of very large online platforms and providers of very large online search engines shall make the reports referred to in this article publicly available at the latest by two months after the date of the conclusion of each reporting period.

4. The transparency reports of providers of intermediary services, providers of hosting services, providers of online platforms, providers of very large online platforms and providers of very large online search engines shall be based on the templates set out in Annex I to this Regulation as of 1 July 2025.

<a id="art_3"></a>

### art_3

Article 3

1. Providers of intermediary services, providers of hosting services, providers of online platforms, and providers of very large online platforms and of very large online search engines shall retain the transparency reports for at least five years after their publication.

2. Transparency reports, including all published versions, shall remain publicly available for the entire duration of the retention period.

<a id="art_4"></a>

### art_4

Article 4

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.
