Where an installation is not covered by the EU ETS in accordance with point 1 of Annex I to Directive 2003/87/EC, the national administrator shall set the corresponding operator account to excluded status for the duration of the exclusion.’;(b)paragraph 7 is replaced by the following:‘7. ### art_2

Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the day of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.

Article 1, points (2), (3), (5), (8) and (12), and Annex I, Annex III and Annex V, point (1), shall apply from 1 January 2025.
