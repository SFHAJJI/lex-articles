### art_1

Article 1

The Annex to Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/1675 shall be amended in accordance with the Annex to this Regulation.

<a id="art_2"></a>

### art_2

Article 2

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication in the Official Journal of the European Union.
